Panini Q

The quantum-simulation realization backend of PANINI: substrates that programs can be realised on and validated against.

Where it sits

Front endILM and the language parsers of tajziya, Sanskrit first.
MiddlewarePANINI's own front ends and the PANINI language.
Core PANINICommon semantics plus decorators.
Realization backendsPanini Q: an oscillator substrate, the PEDLER automaton on it, and a quantum bridge that checks the classical dynamics.
